QUESTIONS, ANSWERED

Good to know.

Can AI detectors be 100% accurate?+

No. Detection is an evolving signal, not proof. Verity is designed to show uncertainty and supporting evidence so people can apply judgment.

What happens to my uploads?+

In this interface, files are only selected locally. In production, connect a secure processing service with clear retention and deletion controls.

Can I use Verity for moderation?+

Yes—with human review, calibrated thresholds, audit logs, and an appeal path. Automated detection should support moderation, never silently replace it.
